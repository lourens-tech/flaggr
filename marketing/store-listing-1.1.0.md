# Store listing text, Flagrr 1.1.0 (approved by the owner, 25 Nov 2026)

Written to the Brand Kit: Flagrr Cash is never money (no wallet, balance, cash back or "points"); no member or club counts; no real club names; rates and rewards are set per club. Every feature line was checked against `docs/Member-App-Features.md`.

Why replace the live text: the current App Store description says "more birdies for your wallet", "Cash in your balance", "pro shop credit" and "the more you play and spend, the higher your tier", which break the Brand Kit or are not how the app works. It also describes a "Digital Club Card", which is not in the app's feature list (unverified, so it is left out here).

**Description** (max 4000)

Scan the slip. Stack Flagrr Cash.

Flagrr is the rewards app for your golf club. Scan your receipts from the halfway house, pro shop or the 19th, earn Flagrr Cash and redeem it for rewards your club picks.

SCAN AND EARN
Point your camera at the slip, or upload a photo of it, and Flagrr reads it for you. Every receipt from your club earns Flagrr Cash. Your club sets how much.

BONUSES
Keep your weekly streak going, collect your birthday reward and refer your four-ball for bonus Flagrr Cash.

CLIMB THE TIERS
Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum. Earn enough Flagrr Cash in a quarter to move up, and higher tiers earn more on every receipt. Tiers are for verified members of your club.

REDEEM AT YOUR CLUB
Rounds, cart hire, pro shop vouchers and more, chosen by your club. Redeem in the app and show the voucher's QR code at the club. Sorted.

EVERY HOLE, MAPPED
On courses Flagrr has mapped, play hole by hole with distances to the green, a suggested club and a scorecard that keeps your score as you go.

KNOW YOUR GAME
My Golf shows your Stableford average, your handicap and the holes that bite, from every round you keep.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Search the Help Center, contact your club and send feedback, all from your profile. Light and dark mode included.

Your club needs to be on Flagrr to earn and redeem. Flagrr Cash is a reward from your club: it is redeemed for rewards in the app and is not money.
