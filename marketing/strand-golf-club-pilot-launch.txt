STRAND GOLF CLUB x FLAGRR

Your golf is about to start paying you back.

We're trying something new at the club. From Friday 25 September, Strand Golf
Club is piloting Flagrr — a loyalty app built specifically for golf clubs and
the people who play them.

Green fees, the pro shop, the halfway house, a round of drinks after a good
back nine — snap the receipt and it turns into Flagrr Cash you spend right
back here at Strand.


STEP ONE: DOWNLOAD THE APP

Sign up takes about a minute. Just pick Strand Golf Club as your home club.

  iPhone:  https://apps.apple.com/za/app/flagrr/id6796048910
  Android: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flagrr.loyalty&hl=en


HOW IT WORKS

  1. Play & scan — photograph your receipt from the club: rounds, pro shop,
     kitchen, bar.
  2. Earn Flagrr Cash — the app reads it and credits your balance
     automatically. No cards, no stamps.
  3. Redeem at the club — swap it for a reward and show the QR voucher at the
     counter.


THE MORE YOU PLAY, THE MORE IT PAYS

  * Four tiers — Bronze to Platinum. Climb them by earning Flagrr Cash through
    the quarter. Platinum earns at 1.7x on everything you scan at Strand.
  * Weekly streaks. Play at least one round a week and every four unbroken
    weeks drops a bonus into your balance.
  * A birthday bonus waiting in the app on the day, and a quarterly bar
    voucher once you reach Gold.
  * Rewards worth having: 9 and 18 hole rounds, cart hire, range time, a
    session with the pro, and pro shop, kitchen or bar vouchers.


ONE MORE THING

This is a pilot, and Strand is the first club on it. Your Flagrr Cash is real
and yours to spend — and we genuinely want to hear what's clunky. Something
not working? Tap Give Feedback under Profile > Support and pick "Bug Found" —
it goes straight to the Flagrr team. For anything about your points or
rewards, use Contact Your Club in the same menu, or speak to anyone in the pro
shop. We'll be adjusting as we go.

See you on the first tee,
The team at Strand Golf Club
