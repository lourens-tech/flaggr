Strand Golf Club × Flagrr

Your golf is about to start paying you back.

We’re trying something new at the club. From Friday 25 September, Strand Golf Club is piloting Flagrr — a loyalty app built specifically for golf clubs and the people who play them.

Green fees, the pro shop, the halfway house, a round of drinks after a good back nine — snap the receipt and it turns into Flagrr Cash you spend right back here at Strand.

Step one

Download the app

Scan the code for your phone — or tap the button under it if you’re reading this on your phone already. Sign up takes about a minute; just pick Strand Golf Club as your home club.

How it works
1

Play & scan

Photograph your receipt from the club — rounds, pro shop, kitchen, bar.
2

Earn Flagrr Cash

The app reads it and credits your balance automatically. No cards, no stamps.
3

Redeem at the club

Swap it for a reward and show the QR voucher at the counter.

The more you play, the more it pays
• Four tiers — Bronze to Platinum. Climb them by earning Flagrr Cash through the quarter. Platinum earns at 1.7× on everything you scan at Strand.
• Weekly streaks. Play at least one round a week and every four unbroken weeks drops a bonus into your balance.
• A birthday bonus waiting in the app on the day, and a quarterly bar voucher once you reach Gold.
• Rewards worth having: 9 and 18 hole rounds, cart hire, range time, a session with the pro, and pro shop, kitchen or bar vouchers.

One more thing

This is a pilot, and Strand is the first club on it. Your Flagrr Cash is real and yours to spend — and we genuinely want to hear what’s clunky. Something not working? Tap Give Feedback under Profile → Support and pick Bug Found — it goes straight to the Flagrr team. For anything about your points or rewards, use Contact Your Club in the same menu, or speak to anyone in the pro shop. We’ll be adjusting as we go.
