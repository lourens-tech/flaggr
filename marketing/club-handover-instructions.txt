FLAGRR PILOT ANNOUNCEMENT — HOW TO SEND IT
Strand Golf Club · pilot starts Friday 25 September

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  flagrr-announcement.html         The email itself.
  flagrr-announcement-INLINE.html  Same email, images built in. Use this one
                                   for the copy-and-paste method below.
  flagrr-announcement.txt          Plain-text version + the subject line.
  qr-app-store.png                 iPhone download code (also fine for print).
  qr-play-store.png                Android download code (also fine for print).

Nothing needs filling in. No names, no dates, no placeholders — it's ready
as it stands.

SUBJECT LINE

  Strand Golf Club is piloting Flagrr — your rounds start paying you back


METHOD 1 — IF YOU SEND THROUGH A MAILING PLATFORM
(Mailchimp, Brevo, Everlytic, Zoho Campaigns, Mailerlite, and similar)

  1. Create a new campaign and choose the template option called
     "Code your own", "Paste in code", "Custom HTML" or "Import HTML" —
     the wording differs by platform.
  2. Open flagrr-announcement.html in Notepad or TextEdit, select all,
     copy, and paste it into that box.
  3. If there's a separate plain-text tab, paste flagrr-announcement.txt
     into it. (This helps the mail reach inboxes rather than spam.)
  4. Send a test to yourself FIRST. Open it on a phone and check that the
     logo and both QR codes appear.
  5. Send to the member list.


METHOD 2 — IF YOU SEND FROM GMAIL OR OUTLOOK DIRECTLY

  1. Double-click flagrr-announcement-INLINE.html so it opens in your web
     browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
  2. Click anywhere on the page, then press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac) to
     select everything, and Ctrl+C (Cmd+C) to copy.
  3. Start a new email, click in the message body, and press Ctrl+V
     (Cmd+V) to paste. The whole design comes across, images and all.
  4. Paste the subject line from the top of this page into the subject box.
  5. Send it to yourself first and check it on a phone.
  6. Put the member addresses in BCC, not TO. Members should not see each
     other's email addresses.


BEFORE THE MEMBER SEND — CHECK THESE

  [ ] The Flagrr logo and both QR codes show up in the test email.
  [ ] Scanning the iPhone code on an iPhone opens the App Store listing.
  [ ] Scanning the Android code on an Android phone opens Google Play.
  [ ] Member addresses are in BCC.


THE QR CODES

The two PNG files work for more than email. They're made at the highest
error-correction setting, so they still scan when printed or partly
obscured — fine for a poster in the clubhouse, a card at the pro shop till,
or the back of a scorecard.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

Broken image icons where the logo and codes should be usually means the
email was sent before the images were published. Speak to the Flagrr team
rather than sending it on to members.
