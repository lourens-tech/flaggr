SENDING THE FLAGRR ANNOUNCEMENT FROM CLUBMASTER
Strand Golf Club · pilot starts Friday 25 September


START HERE: WHICH KIND OF EDITOR DO YOU HAVE?

Open ClubMaster and start a new bulk email to members, as you normally
would. Look along the toolbar above the typing area.

Do you see a button marked < > , or a menu item called "Source code",
"Source", "HTML" or "Code view"?

    YES  ->  Follow METHOD A. It gives the best-looking result.
    NO   ->  Follow METHOD B. It works in any editor.

If you are not sure, try METHOD A first. If the design does not appear,
nothing is broken — undo, and use METHOD B instead.


SUBJECT LINE (both methods)

    Strand Golf Club is piloting Flagrr — your rounds start paying you back


-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD A — YOUR EDITOR HAS AN HTML / SOURCE VIEW
-------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open the file "flagrr-announcement.html" in Notepad (right-click it,
     choose Open with > Notepad). It will look like a page of computer
     code. That is correct.
  2. Click once inside it, press Ctrl+A to select all, then Ctrl+C to copy.
  3. Back in ClubMaster, click the < > (or "Source code") button.
  4. Delete anything already in the box, then press Ctrl+V to paste.
  5. Save or close that box. The design should now appear in the editor.
  6. Skip to BEFORE YOU SEND, below.

Do NOT paste this code into the normal typing area. It only works inside
the source/HTML box.


-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD B — ORDINARY EDITOR (NO HTML BUTTON)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

You will type or paste the words, and insert three pictures where marked.

  1. Open "email-text.txt" (double-click; it opens in Notepad).
  2. Copy all of it into the ClubMaster typing area.
  3. You will see three lines that look like this:

         [INSERT PICTURE: image-1-header.png]

     For each one: delete that line, leave the cursor on the empty line,
     then use ClubMaster's "Insert image" button to upload and place the
     matching picture file from this folder.

  4. Make the headline "Your golf is about to start paying you back."
     larger and bold, if you like. Everything else can stay as it is.
  5. Check that the two download links are clickable. If they are not,
     highlight the words "App Store" / "Google Play" and use the link
     button to attach the web addresses written next to them.


-------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU SEND — BOTH METHODS
-------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ ] Send a test to yourself first. ClubMaster usually has a "Send test"
      option; if not, send it to your own address only.
  [ ] Open that test ON A MOBILE PHONE. Most members will read it there.
  [ ] Check the Flagrr logo and both QR codes actually appear.
  [ ] Ask someone with an iPhone to scan the iPhone code, and someone with
      an Android phone to scan the Android code. Each should open the app
      store page.
  [ ] Only then send to the full member list.

clubhouse board, a card at the pro shop till, or the back of a scorecard.


-------------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU GET STUCK
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Send the test email to the Flagrr team and we will tell you whether it
looks right before it goes to members. Better a slow send than a broken
one landing in 400 inboxes.
