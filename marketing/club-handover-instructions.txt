SENDING THE FLAGRR ANNOUNCEMENT FROM CLUBMASTER
Strand Golf Club · pilot starts Friday 25 September


There are three ways to do this. Pick ONE. They all reach members with the
same message — they differ only in how the design travels.

    METHOD 1  Paste the words, attach the picture.   Easiest. Always works.
    METHOD 2  Paste the words, place 3 pictures.     Design sits in the email.
    METHOD 3  Paste the design code.                 Best looking, if offered.

If you have never done this before, use METHOD 1.


SUBJECT LINE (all three methods)

    Strand Golf Club is piloting Flagrr — your rounds start paying you back


-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1 — PASTE THE WORDS, ATTACH THE PICTURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Open "email-text.txt" (double-click it; it opens in Notepad).
  2. Click inside it, press Ctrl+A to select all, then Ctrl+C to copy.
  3. In ClubMaster, start your bulk email to members and click in the
     typing area. Press Ctrl+V to paste.
  4. Use ClubMaster's attach button (usually a paperclip) and attach
     "flagrr-announcement.jpg" from this folder.
  5. Go to BEFORE YOU SEND, below.

The email works on its own even if a member never opens the attachment —
the download links are in the words you pasted. The picture is the nicely
designed version for those who do open it.


-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2 — PASTE THE WORDS, PLACE 3 PICTURES
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Use this if you would rather the design appeared in the email itself
instead of as an attachment.

  1. Open "email-text-with-pictures.txt" and copy all of it into the
     ClubMaster typing area.
  2. You will see three lines that look like this:

         [INSERT PICTURE: image-1-header.png]

     For each one: delete that line, leave the cursor on the blank line,
     and use ClubMaster's "Insert image" button to place the matching
     picture file from this folder.
  3. Make the headline "Your golf is about to start paying you back."
     bigger and bold if you like. The rest can stay as it is.
  4. Check the two download links are clickable. If not, highlight the
     words "App Store" / "Google Play" and use the link button to attach
     the web addresses printed next to them.


-------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 3 — PASTE THE DESIGN CODE
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Only possible if ClubMaster's editor has a button marked < > , or a menu
item called "Source code", "Source", "HTML" or "Code view". If you don't
see one, use METHOD 1 instead — nothing is wrong.

  1. Right-click "flagrr-announcement.html" and choose Open with > Notepad.
     It will look like a page of computer code. That is correct.
  2. Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C to copy all of it.
  3. In ClubMaster, click the < > (or "Source code") button.
  4. Delete anything already in that box, then press Ctrl+V.
  5. Save or close the box. The design should appear in the editor.

Do NOT paste this code into the normal typing area — it only works inside
the source box.


-------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE YOU SEND — ALL METHODS
-------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ ] Send a test to yourself first. ClubMaster usually has a "Send test"
      option; if not, send to your own address only.
  [ ] Open that test ON A MOBILE PHONE. Most members read email there.
  [ ] Check the pictures appear (or the attachment opens).
  [ ] Ask someone with an iPhone to tap the App Store link, and someone
      with an Android phone to tap the Google Play link. Each should open
      the app store page.
  [ ] Only then send to the full member list.

IF YOU GET STUCK
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Send the test email to the Flagrr team and we will tell you whether it
looks right before it goes to members. Better a slow send than a broken
one landing in 400 inboxes.
