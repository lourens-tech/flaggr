Support

Need help with Flagrr?

The fastest way to get help is directly in the app:

  • Open Flagrr and go to Profile
  • Tap Contact Support or Help Center under the Support section
  • Send us a message — our team replies directly in the app, and you'll get a push notification when we respond

Common questions

Why wasn't my receipt approved? Receipts can be declined if the image is unclear, the purchase isn't eligible, or it was already submitted. Try rescanning with a clearer photo of a valid receipt.

How do I redeem Flagrr Cash? Go to the Rewards tab, choose a reward, and tap Redeem Now. Your voucher will be ready to present at your club.

How do I delete my account? See our account deletion instructions.

Still need help?
